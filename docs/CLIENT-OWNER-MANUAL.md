# USFL website — Owner's manual

This manual is for the people who run the USFL website. You do not need to be technical.

The website has its own **editor**. You sign in, see the real website, click what you want to change, and change it. Your changes are saved as a **draft** automatically. Visitors see nothing new until you press **Publish**. Every published version is kept, so anything can be put back.

## 1. Signing in

1. Go to **`https://<your-domain>/admin`**.
2. Enter your email and password, then choose **Sign in**.
3. The first time, you sign in with a one-time password from the site owner and are asked to **choose a new password** (at least 10 characters).

To sign out, choose **Sign out** at the bottom left. To change your password, choose your email address at the bottom left.

Forgotten your password? Ask an owner to choose **Reset password** for you under **Accounts**.

## 2. A tour of the editor

**The left column** lists everything you can manage:

| Group | Items |
|---|---|
| Website | **Overview** (start here), **Pages**, **Case studies**, **Capabilities**, **Insights**, **Connected system**, **Company facts** |
| Media | **Media library** (images and videos), **Films & cinematics** |
| Design | **Colours, type & spacing**, **Motion**, **Branding** |
| Site | **Navigation & footer**, **Site settings & SEO**, **Inbox** (form messages), **History & versions**, **Accounts** |

**The top bar** always shows **Live site** (opens the public website in a new tab) and **Publish**. The Publish button shows a number when draft changes are waiting.

**The page editor** (Pages → a page) has three parts:

- **Left — Sections.** The page's sections in order. Each row has an eye (show or hide the section), arrows (move it up or down), and a handle for dragging. The row at the top opens **Page settings** (title, address, search & sharing). **Add section** is at the bottom.
- **Middle — the real page.** This is the actual website, showing your draft. Above it are **Desktop / Tablet / Mobile** and **Edit / Browse**.
  - In **Edit** mode, move the mouse over the page and everything you can change is outlined and labelled.
    - **Click** text to open it in the panel on the right.
    - **Double-click** text to type straight onto the page. Press Enter or click away to keep the change, or Esc to cancel. For headlines with several lines, each line is on its own row.
    - **Click an image** to replace it.
  - In **Browse** mode, links work, so you can move around the site.
- **Right — the inspector.** The settings for what you selected, in tabs: **Content** (words, buttons, lists), **Layout** (background, alignment, image side and shape, columns, spacing, hide on phones, tablets or desktops), **Media** (images and films), **Motion**, **Form** (on the contact section) and **Section** (its name, and hide).

The top bar in the page editor also shows the page's status (**Published**, **Draft changes** or **New — not published**), the save status (**Draft saved**), **Undo** and **Redo**, and **Preview**.

## 3. Drafts, preview, publish and undo

**Saving.** Everything saves itself as a draft about a second after you stop typing. The top bar says **Draft saved**. Nobody outside the editor sees drafts.

**Previewing.**

- The page in the middle of the editor *is* the preview: it is the real site showing your draft. Use **Desktop / Tablet / Mobile** to check each size.
- **Preview** in the top bar shows the page full-size, without any editor controls. You can still switch between Desktop, Tablet and Mobile there.

**Publishing.**

1. Choose **Publish** in the top bar. A panel lists every page or setting with draft changes. Choose **What changed** under any of them to see each change, before and after.
2. If something would break the site (for example, two pages with the same address, or a menu link to a page that does not exist), it appears under **Fix before publishing**, and publishing waits until it is fixed. Click a problem to go straight to it.
3. Optionally, write a **Note for the history**, such as "Spring campaign".
4. Choose **Publish to the live site**. Visitors see the changes straight away.

**Undoing.** There are four ways to undo, from smallest to biggest:

| Situation | Use |
|---|---|
| A change you just made | **Undo** (top bar, or Ctrl/⌘ + Z when you are not typing in a field) |
| A page or setting you want back as it is live | Publish panel → **Discard** next to it |
| An earlier draft of one page or setting | The clock icon (**Earlier drafts of this**). Pick a moment, see the differences, then **Restore this draft**. |
| The whole site as it was on a date | **History & versions** → **Restore into draft** (to check it first), or **Restore & publish** |

Deleted pages, case studies and media can always be brought back: from the Pages list (**Restore**), from History, or from the media **Trash**.

## 4. Your first 10 edits

Try these in order the first time you use the editor. They take about 15 minutes. At the end, open **Publish** and choose **Discard all** if you do not want to keep them. None of them reaches the live site until you publish.

| # | Edit | How |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Change the home page headline on the page itself | Pages → **Home**. Double-click the big headline. Change a word, then click away. |
| 2 | Change the text under it in the panel | Click the paragraph under the headline. Its field lights up on the right. Edit it there. |
| 3 | Replace a picture | Pages → **About**. Click the picture at the top, then **Drop files here** (or pick an existing image) and **Use this image**. |
| 4 | Hide a section and bring it back | In the Sections list, click the eye on **Proof** (home page). Click it again to show it. |
| 5 | Move a section | Use the arrows on a section, or drag it by its handle. |
| 6 | Add a section | **Add section** → **Statement**. Type your lines in the panel. |
| 7 | Change a layout | With your statement selected, open **Layout**: set **Alignment** to centre and **Hide on** → **Phones**. Switch the preview to **Mobile** to see it disappear there. |
| 8 | Change the accent colour | **Colours, type & spacing** → **Accent (signal)**. The contrast number next to text colours tells you whether they are still easy to read. |
| 9 | Calm the motion | **Motion** → **Motion preset** → **Subtle**. |
| 10 | Review and publish (or discard) | **Publish** → look at **What changed** → **Publish to the live site**, or **Discard all**. |

## 5. How-to guides

Each guide says **where** to go, **what** to do, **why** you might, the **result**, how to **preview** it, and how to **undo** it.

### Pages

#### Edit text on a page
- **Where:** Pages → the page.
- **What:** Double-click the text on the page and type, or click it and edit the highlighted field on the right. Wrap words in `*asterisks*` to set them in the italic serif, as in `We build *customer* *growth* *systems.*`
- **Why:** Everyday copy changes.
- **Result:** The draft updates and the preview reloads within a second or two.
- **Preview:** In the middle of the editor, at every screen size.
- **Undo:** **Undo**, or discard the page's changes in the Publish panel.

#### Create a new page
- **Where:** **Pages** → **New page**.
- **What:** Type a **Page title**. The **Address** is suggested for you. Choose a starting point: **Blank page**, **Landing page**, **Campaign page**, **Services page** or **Long-form page**, or copy the sections of an existing page. Then choose **Create page**. In **Page settings**, switch on **Published on the site** when the page is ready.
- **Why:** Campaigns, landing pages and new services.
- **Result:** A new page in the site's design, with placeholder content for you to replace. It stays off the live site until it is switched on and published.
- **Preview:** The editor shows it straight away, even before you switch it on.
- **Undo:** **Delete page** (in Page settings), or discard it in the Publish panel.

#### Duplicate, rename, change the address, hide or delete a page
- **Where:** Pages → the page → **Page settings** (the top row of the Sections list).
- **What:**
  - Change **Page title** or **Address**.
  - Switch **Published on the site** off to hide the page.
  - **Duplicate page** makes an unpublished copy.
  - **Delete page** asks you to type "delete" to confirm.
  - On the **Pages** list, the **On the site** switch hides or shows a page, and dragging sets the order.
- **Why:** Tidy up, restructure, retire pages.
- **Result:** When you change an address, update any menu link that pointed to the old one (publishing will remind you). To keep old links working, add a **Redirect** (see below).
- **Undo:** Change it back, **Restore** it from "Removed in the draft" on the Pages list, or use History.

#### Choose a different home page
- **Where:** **Pages** → **Make home page** on the page you want.
- **What:** Give the current home page a new address when asked.
- **Result:** The chosen page is shown at `/` once you publish.
- **Undo:** Make the old page the home page again.

#### Send an old address to a new page (redirect)
- **Where:** **Site settings & SEO** → **Redirects** → **Add redirect**.
- **What:** Fill in **Old address** (for example `/old-offer`) and **New address**. Leave **Permanent (301)** on unless the move is temporary.
- **Result:** Visitors and search engines following the old address land on the new page.
- **Undo:** Remove the redirect.

### Sections and layout

#### Add, duplicate, reorder, hide or delete a section
- **Where:** Pages → the page → the Sections list.
- **What:**
  - **Add section** opens a gallery grouped into Story scenes, Lists, Content, Media and Page. The new section goes below the selected one.
  - In the panel, the copy icon duplicates the section and the bin icon deletes it.
  - The eye hides the section. Arrows and dragging change the order.
- **Why:** Change how the page tells its story. For example, move *Selected work* above *Capabilities*.
- **Result:** The page's flow changes. Every section keeps the site's typography and motion.
- **Undo:** **Undo**, the eye, or discard.

#### Change a section's layout
- **Where:** Select the section → **Layout** tab.
- **What:** Depending on the section:
  - **Background** (dark, dark secondary or white);
  - **Alignment** and **Size**;
  - **Image side** and **Image shape**;
  - **Media position** (right, left or full width);
  - **Columns on desktop**, **Height** and **Text position**;
  - **Spacing above and below** (as designed, tighter, more space, none, or no space above);
  - **Hide on** phones, tablets or desktops.
- **Why:** Fine-tune the rhythm without leaving the design system.
- **Result:** The layout changes within the site's grid, so nothing can overflow or stretch.
- **Undo:** Set it back, or **Undo**.

#### Change the colour-change transitions between sections
- **Where:** Select a **Transition** section → **Motion** and **Layout** tabs.
- **What:** Choose a **Style** (circle, diagonal, rise, shutter, bars, fade, or cut for no animation), the **Scroll length**, **From** and **To** backgrounds, and where the circle starts from.
- **Result:** That transition changes. To change all of them at once, use **Motion** → **Effects** → **Colour-change transitions**.

### Words, buttons and links

#### Change a button
- **Where:** Select the section with the button → **Content**.
- **What:** Each button has a label and a link. Links to your own pages start with `/` (suggestions appear as you type). Links to other websites start with `https://`.
- **Result:** The label and destination change. The menu button, **Start a conversation**, is under **Navigation & footer** → **Menu button**.

#### Edit the menu, dropdowns and the mobile menu
- **Where:** **Navigation & footer**.
- **What:**
  - Under **Main menu**, add, rename, reorder, hide or remove links.
  - Give a link **Dropdown links (desktop)** to make a dropdown.
  - **Menu button** is the highlighted button on the right.
  - **Mobile menu** has its own options.
- **Result:** The desktop menu, mobile menu and the footer's menu column update together.
- **Undo:** Discard, or **Undo**.

#### Edit the footer
- **Where:** **Navigation & footer** → **Footer**.
- **What:**
  - Edit the **Eyebrow**, **Heading** and **Columns** (each with links or text; switch on **Show contact details** to show the contact details from Site settings).
  - Edit **Legal links**, **Copyright** (`{year}` becomes the current year) and **Bottom-right text**.

### Images and videos

#### Upload images and videos
- **Where:** **Media library**, or any image field → **Replace**.
- **What:** Drag files onto **Drop files here**, or choose them from your computer.
  - Images: JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF or GIF, up to 30 MB. They are resized, optimised and made into a phone-sized copy automatically.
  - Videos: MP4 or WebM, up to 400 MB.
- **Why:** New photography and films.
- **Result:** The files appear in the library, ready to use anywhere.

#### Replace an image
- **Where:** Click the image on the page, or use the image field in the panel.
- **What:** Pick an image and choose **Use this image**.
- **Result:** The new image takes the old one's place with the same shape, crop behaviour, motion and reveal. It is never stretched.
- **Undo:** **Undo**, or pick the previous image.

#### Describe an image, set its focal point, caption or "Illustrative" label
- **Where:** **Media library** → click the image.
- **What:**
  - Click the most important part of the picture to set the **focal point**. It stays in view however the image is cropped.
  - Write a **Description (alt text)** for people using screen readers.
  - Add a **Caption** if you want one.
  - Switch on **Mark as illustrative** for artwork that is not a documentary photograph.
  - **Fit** chooses whether the image fills its frame or shows whole.
  - **Used on** shows every page that uses the image.
- **Result:** These settings apply everywhere the image is used. They are part of the draft, so publish to make them live.

#### Organise and delete media
- **Where:** **Media library**.
- **What:**
  - Search by name, and filter by **Images**, **Videos** or **Trash**.
  - Give an image a **Folder** to group it.
  - **Move to trash** warns you first if the file is still used, and lists where.
  - The **Trash** tab can **Restore** a file, or **Delete permanently**.
- **Note:** Files that came with the site cannot be deleted, but you can stop using them anywhere.

#### Replace or add a film (including the hero film)
- **Where:** **Films & cinematics**.
- **What:** Each film has a **Name** that sections use to place it.
  - For a video: choose **Kind** → **Video file**, then pick the **Video (MP4)** (and optionally the WebM and phone versions) from the library, a **Poster**, and a **Description for screen readers**.
  - **Playback** can be loop, play once, play once after the intro, or controlled by the scene.
  - **Switched on** off shows the poster instead, so the layout never breaks.
  - For a moving photograph, choose **Kind** → **Photo with a camera move** and set **From** and **To** (zoom and pan).
- **Result:** Every section using that film name shows the new film. To use a different film in one section, choose it in the section's **Media** tab.
- **Note:** The hero film is called `hero-plunge`. It is Image 01 brought to life (see `docs/CINEMATIC-GUIDE.md` for how films are made).

### Case studies, capabilities, insights and the system

#### Add or edit a case study
- **Where:** **Case studies** → **New case study** (or click one).
- **What:**
  - Fill in the **Title**, **Address name**, **Display title lines**, **Summary**, **Maps to capabilities**, images or a film, **Story layout**, **Facts**, then the story itself.
  - Switch **Hide from the site** off when it is ready.
  - Drag case studies on the list to reorder them.
- **Why:** New work.
- **Result:** The case study gets its own page. It appears on Work with its "Maps to: …" line, and on the capability pages it maps to.
- **Important:** Publish only facts, figures, client names and quotes that are confirmed and approved. Empty fields are simply not shown.

#### Choose the case studies on the home page
- **Where:** Pages → **Home** → the **Selected work** section.
- **What:** **Lead case study** → **Case study**, and **Two more case studies**.

#### Edit capabilities, insights, the connected system and company facts
- **Capabilities:** name, statement, image, services, principles, **System stages it serves** and **Related insights**.
- **Insights:** articles built from blocks: paragraph, heading, pull quote, list, formula, numbered step and call-out.
- **Connected system:** the stages Strategy → Experience → Data → Technology → Automation → Growth. Rename them, reorder them, hide them, or add supporting text, related capabilities and case studies. The scene on the home page follows your list and keeps its single connected line.
- **Company facts:** *Who we work with*, *Why USFL*, the marquee words and the partnership. Several sections reuse these.

### Design

#### Colours
- **Where:** **Colours, type & spacing** → **Colours**.
- **What:** Change any colour, or apply a preset: **USFL Signal (current)** or **Copper & Paper**. Presets change the draft only.
- **Protection:** If a text colour would be hard to read on its background, the site automatically uses black or white instead, and publishing lists it under **Worth a look**.

#### Typography, spacing and corners
- **Where:** **Colours, type & spacing** → **Typography**, **Spacing**, **Layout**, **Borders & corners**.
- **What:** Fonts, sizes, weights, line heights, label and button styles, page side padding, overall section spacing, maximum content width and corner rounding.
- **Tip:** Change one thing at a time and check Desktop and Mobile.

#### Logo, favicon and sharing image
- **Where:** **Branding**.
- **What:** Keep the built-in usfl wordmark, or upload a logo file (and a version for light backgrounds). Also set the browser icon, the theme colour and the social sharing image.

#### Motion
- **Where:** **Motion**.
- **What:**
  - **Motion preset**: None, Subtle, Standard (as designed), Cinematic or Dramatic.
  - **Speed** and **Intensity**, plus text, block and image reveal styles.
  - **Effects**: the intro, the page-change curtain, the image that grows into the next page, colour-change transitions, the cursor glow, magnetic buttons, image hover movement, film grain, the progress line, and the navigation turning dark on white sections.
  - Each section can also set its own motion in its **Motion** tab.
- **Always true:** visitors who ask their device for reduced motion get calm, still layouts (keep **Respect visitors who ask for reduced motion** on). Text only ever slides or fades into place and always ends crisp and solid.

### Search engines and sharing

- **Per page:** Page settings → **Search & sharing**. Set the title, description, social title, description and image, the canonical address, and whether search engines should index the page.
- **Case studies, capabilities and insights** have their own **Search & sharing** group.
- **Site-wide defaults:** **Site settings & SEO** → **Default search & sharing**.
- The server builds a sitemap (`/sitemap.xml`) and search-engine rules (`/robots.txt`) automatically from what is published.

### Contact form and messages

- **The form:** Pages → **Contact** → the **Contact** section → **Form** tab.
  - **Show the contact form** switches it on or off.
  - **Form fields** lets you add, remove, reorder or rename fields, and mark them required or optional. A field can be a single line, an email address, a phone number, a longer message, or a list of choices.
  - **Form messages** sets the button and the thank-you, error and consent text.
- **Messages:** they arrive in **Inbox** (owners and admins). You can reply by email, download them all as a spreadsheet file (CSV), or delete them.
- **Forwarding:** to also receive messages elsewhere (for example by email through Zapier or Make), put a webhook address under **Site settings & SEO** → **Contact** → **Also send form messages to**.
- **Contact details:** the email address, phone number and street address under **Site settings & SEO** → **Contact** appear only once they are filled in. Leave them empty until they are confirmed.

### Analytics and privacy notice

- **Where:** **Site settings & SEO** → **Analytics** and **Privacy & cookies**.
- **What:** Add a Plausible domain or a Google tag ID. With **Ask for consent before analytics** on, analytics only start after a visitor accepts the notice. Write the notice text and link your privacy policy.

## 6. Accounts and roles

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything, including adding and removing accounts |
| **Admin** | Edit, publish, restore versions, delete media, read the inbox, change site-wide design and settings |
| **Editor** | Edit pages, case studies and content as drafts, and upload media. **Cannot publish.** Editors see "Ask an admin to review and publish them" instead. |

Owners manage accounts under **Accounts**:

- **Add account** shows a one-time password to hand over privately.
- The **Role** list changes what someone can do.
- **Disable** blocks someone from signing in without removing them.
- **Reset password** gives them a new one-time password.
- **Remove** deletes the account.

The site always keeps at least one owner.

## 8. Writing conventions

- **Italic serif words:** wrap them in asterisks, for example `*growth*`. Several words: `*customer growth systems.*`
- **Headlines with several lines:** each row is one line on the page.
- **Links:** your own pages start with `/`. Other websites start with `https://`. Links that could run scripts are removed automatically when saved.
- **Image descriptions:** say what the picture shows, in one sentence.
- **Claims:** publish only facts, figures, client names, awards and quotes that USFL has confirmed. Leave a field empty rather than estimating.

## 9. Good habits and what to leave alone

- **Publish in small batches, with a note.** It makes History easy to read.
- **Check Mobile** before publishing big changes.
- **Hide instead of delete** when you might want something back.
- **Section names** (the *Section* tab) are used for links like `/about#why`. Avoid renaming them once people link to them.
- **Address prefixes (advanced)** in Site settings change every case study, capability and insight address at once. Leave them alone unless you really mean it.
- **Scroll length** on the big home-page scenes is part of the pacing. Small changes (0.8–1.3) are fine.
- The design's breakpoint (where the layout switches to mobile) is a developer setting and is not in the editor.
- The server's files, database and backups are for your developer or host. Nothing in normal website management needs them.

## 10. Troubleshooting

| What you see | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Publish** is greyed out | A problem is listed under **Fix before publishing** | Click the problem. It takes you to the page or setting to fix. |
| "Changed by someone else — reload" | Two people edited the same page at once | Reload the page, check their change, then make yours again. |
| "Not saved" | The connection dropped | Keep the tab open. The next change retries. If it keeps happening, check your internet connection. |
| The preview looks half-animated | Scroll scenes are the real site, and they play as you scroll | Scroll the preview a little, or use **Preview**. |
| An image looks badly cropped | Its focal point is centred | **Media library** → the image → click the important part. |
| The video does not play on a phone | The phone is in low-power or data-saving mode | Expected: the poster shows instead. |
| Nothing moves | The Motion preset is **None**, or the visitor's device asks for reduced motion | Check **Motion**. |
| A colour looks different from what you picked | Contrast protection replaced an unreadable text colour | Pick a colour with more contrast (see the number next to text colours). |
| A page says "not found" in the preview | Its address is empty or clashes with another page | Check **Page settings** → **Address**. |
| You cannot see Publish, Accounts or Inbox | Your role does not include them | Ask an owner. |
| You are signed out unexpectedly | Sessions last 14 days, and a password reset signs you out everywhere | Sign in again. Drafts are never lost. |
| The contact form does not appear | **Show the contact form** is off | Pages → Contact → Contact section → **Form** tab. |
